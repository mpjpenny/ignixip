Privacy

This page says, in ordinary language, what ignixip does with information. It is not legal advice. Ignix runs the site and is based in the UK. Questions and deletion requests go to hello@ignix.co.uk.

Lookups and tools

IP, DNS, WHOIS, blacklist, ASN and mail-authentication lookups send the address or domain you type to the service that can answer it. That includes public DNS and RDAP, and ignixip's own tools host at tools.ignixip.com, so the page can show a result. Some lookups are logged there so we can run and improve the site. We don't sell that information.

Email header analysis and DMARC aggregate reports are read in your browser. Those files are not uploaded by the page that parses them.

Some pages load a first-party analytics script from analytics.ignix.co.uk, which records a page view. The watch and privacy pages do not.

Watch alerts

The watch form is optional. If you use it, we store the email address and the IP or domain you asked us to watch, plus whether you want blacklist alerts, DNS alerts, or both. We store that only to send these alerts. We don't sell addresses, and we don't add you to a general mailing list.

Alerts are consent-based. Submitting the form asks us to email a confirmation. We only send change alerts after you use that link. If you do not confirm, no watch alerts are sent. We only email when something we are watching has changed. Every alert includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing deletes that watch, including the email address and the target held for it.

A hidden field on the form is there to catch automated signups. People using the form in a browser leave it blank. Please don't fill it in.

You can ask what we hold about an email address and ask us to delete it, whether or not you still have the unsubscribe link. Write to hello@ignix.co.uk from that address.
