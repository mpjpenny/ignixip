DMARC check

Enter a domain to read the TXT and MX records that mail authentication depends on.

DMARC and SPF are published as DNS TXT records. This page loads the domain's TXT and MX records so you can see whether a policy is present. For a plain-English reading of an aggregate report file, use the DMARC report tool.

Questions

What should I look for?

A TXT record starting with v=spf1, and a TXT record at _dmarc.example.com starting with v=DMARC1. This quick form shows TXT records on the domain itself.

Does this parse a DMARC report?

No. Paste an aggregate report into the DMARC report tool. This page is the DNS half of the check.

Why check MX as well?

MX records name the servers that receive mail. A domain with no MX and no SPF is not set up to send or receive mail in the usual way.
